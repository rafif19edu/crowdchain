About

Sixty students live at the maahad from Sunday to Friday. This 8-week fund covers their weekly rice, cooking oil, fresh fish and chicken, vegetables and drinking water. Each Monday one week of funding is released, and we upload the supermarket receipt and the bank transfer slip before the next week can be paid out.
